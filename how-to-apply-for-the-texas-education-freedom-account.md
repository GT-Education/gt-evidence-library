# How to Apply for the Texas Education Freedom Account

To apply for the Texas Education Freedom Account (TEFA), you confirm your eligibility on the official state site, prepare the required documents, and submit an application during the state's enrollment window. Once you receive an award, you choose a participating school and coordinate payment through the program. The one thing to keep front of mind: eligibility, the application window, and funding are all set by the state and can change, so the official site (educationfreedom.texas.gov) is your source of truth, and this article is the plain-English walkthrough. No school can promise you an award.

## Key takeaways
- You apply for TEFA through the official state program, not through a school; eligibility and funding are set by the state.
- The basic process: confirm eligibility, prepare documents, apply in the official window, receive an award, then choose a participating school.
- The 2026-27 award is $10,474 per student, usable for approved education expenses including tuition at participating private and online schools.
- Priority rules and the application window are set by the state and can change each cycle; confirm them on the official site before you rely on them.
- For families weighing an accredited online school, GT reports GT Anywhere tuition is $10,400 per year, at or under the award.

## The Texas Education Freedom Account in plain terms
In short, the Texas Education Freedom Account is the state's education savings account (ESA): for eligible families, Texas puts funds into an account you can spend on approved education expenses, including tuition at participating private and online schools. This article is focused on how to apply. For the fuller background on what TEFA is, how it works, and what it can pay for, see [what is the Texas Education Freedom Account](what-is-the-texas-education-freedom-account).

## Who qualifies, and what it covers
Eligibility is set by the state, and this is the part not to guess about. In general terms, TEFA is for Texas families, and when demand is high the program can use income-priority tiers to decide who is funded first. Funds cover approved education expenses, which typically include tuition at participating schools and often other qualified costs. The exact rules change from cycle to cycle, so treat the following as shapes to confirm, not promises:

- Who qualifies: Texas families who meet the current criteria. [STAT: current eligibility and income-priority tiers per educationfreedom.texas.gov]
- What it covers: approved education expenses, including tuition at participating private and online schools. [STAT: full list of approved expenses per educationfreedom.texas.gov]
- How much: the 2026-27 award is $10,474 per student.

No one outside the state program can tell you for certain that you qualify or that funds are available in a given year, so confirm your status on the official [Texas Education Freedom](https://educationfreedom.texas.gov/) site before you commit to anything.

## Step by step: applying for TEFA
Here is the shape of the process. Confirm every specific on the official site, since dates and requirements are set by the state and can change.

1. Confirm eligibility. Start on the official Texas Education Freedom site and check the current criteria for your family before doing anything else. Do not assume you qualify.
2. Prepare your documents. Gather what the state requires, which commonly includes proof of Texas residency and your child's records. [STAT: exact required documents per educationfreedom.texas.gov]
3. Submit the application in the official window. Applications are accepted during a set enrollment window, and windows can open and close each cycle. [DATA: 2026-27 application window dates per educationfreedom.texas.gov]
4. Receive your award, then choose a participating school. Funding depends on state approval. Once you have an award, you can select a participating private or accredited online school, such as an accredited K-8 online school.
5. Enroll and coordinate payment through the program. Complete enrollment with your chosen school and follow the program's process for directing your award to approved expenses like tuition.

At every step, the official site is the authority. A school can help you understand the process, but it cannot grant eligibility or guarantee funding.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I apply for the Texas Education Freedom Account?
You apply through the official state program: confirm your eligibility on educationfreedom.texas.gov, prepare the required documents, and submit an application during the state's enrollment window. After you receive an award, you choose a participating school and coordinate payment through the program.

### Who qualifies for the Texas Education Freedom Account?
Eligibility is set by the state and can include income-priority tiers when demand is high. In general it is for Texas families who meet the current criteria, but you should not assume you qualify; confirm current eligibility on the official site before you rely on it.

### How much is the Texas ESA award, and what does it cover?
The 2026-27 award is $10,474 per student, usable for approved education expenses including tuition at participating private and online schools. The official program lists exactly what counts.

### Do I need an award before I choose a school?
You need an approved award to use TEFA funds. You can research or apply to schools first, but funding depends on state approval, so confirm your award before you rely on it. Without a current award you can still enroll and pay tuition directly, then apply in a future window.
