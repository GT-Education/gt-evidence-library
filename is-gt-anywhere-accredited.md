# Is GT Anywhere Accredited, and What Does That Mean for Transcripts?

This question is really two questions wearing one coat, and families asking it usually care most
about the second.

The first is formal: what body accredits the school. The second is practical: if we do this for two years
and then move, will a traditional school accept the work, and will my child be placed correctly?

The second question has a partial answer independent of accreditation. GT's model produces an unusually
detailed record: continuous mastery data by subject, MAP results, and documented placement levels. In
practice a receiving school's placement decision often rests more on that evidence than on a transcript.
