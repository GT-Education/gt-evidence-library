# Does GT Anywhere Offer High School, and What Happens After 8th Grade?

GT Anywhere serves K through 8. That is the honest starting point, and it is the first thing to say
to any family whose child is approaching the end of that range.

What GT has described as the pathways after 8th grade:

- **Alpha High (virtual).** GT graduates who qualify for the honors track have a route into Alpha High.
- **Selective private and prep schools.** GT's stated aim is that its 8th graders arrive at high school
  admissions with results well above the typical applicant.
- **Public high school.** GT works with families on course selection to keep the challenge level high.

[NEEDS-FACT: Three things need GT's confirmation before this publishes, because 68 families asked and a vague
answer will read as evasion:
(1) Is a GT high school actually on the roadmap, and is there any date or range GT is willing to state publicly?
(2) What are the exact terms of the Alpha High pathway: who qualifies, is admission guaranteed, is there tuition credit?
(3) What placement or outcome data can GT publish about where its 8th graders have actually gone?]
