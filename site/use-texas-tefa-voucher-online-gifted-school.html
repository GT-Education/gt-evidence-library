Can I Use My Texas TEFA Voucher for an Online Gifted School?

Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

If you've heard that Texas now has education savings accounts and you're wondering whether one could pay for an online gifted school, the short answer is yes. An approved Texas Education Freedom Account (TEFA) can go toward tuition at a participating accredited private school, and that includes an online K–8 gifted program like GT Anywhere. The one thing to keep front of mind: who qualifies, and when the window opens, are decided by the state, not the school. So treat the official Texas Education Freedom site as your source of truth, and this article as the plain-English overview.

What a Texas Education Freedom Account is

A Texas Education Freedom Account is the state's education savings account (ESA) program: eligible families get state funds they can put toward approved education expenses, including private-school tuition. It's part of a national shift, with more than 1.5 million students now in 75 private school-choice programs across 34 states, D.C., and Puerto Rico. For 2026-27, the award is $10,474 per eligible student.

Step by step: using TEFA for an online gifted school

  1. Confirm you're eligible. Check who qualifies for the current cycle on the official Texas Education Freedom site, since the rules can change year to year.
  2. Apply during an open window. Awards are granted in application windows, so note the current dates on the official site.
  3. Get your award approval. The funding depends on state approval, and this is the step that decides whether TEFA can pay your tuition.
  4. Choose a participating accredited school. An accredited online K–8 gifted program (such as GT Anywhere) can receive the funds.
  5. Apply the award to tuition. With an approved award of $10,474 and GT Anywhere tuition at $10,400, an eligible family can cover tuition in full.

Who qualifies, and what to check

Because eligibility is set by the state and not the school, a few honest cautions before you count on anything:

  • Don't assume you qualify. Verify against the current official criteria first.
  • Windows and award amounts can change each cycle; the $10,474 figure is for 2026-27.
  • Approval comes first. You can enroll or apply to a school beforehand, but the funding hinges on an approved award.

The single reliable source for all of this is the official Texas Education Freedom site.

How this applies to GT Anywhere

GT Anywhere is the online version of GT School (the gifted academy of the Alpha School family), an accredited K–8 program on the same mastery-based platform used at the Georgetown campus. Tuition is $10,400/year, deliberately priced at or under the TEFA award so that, for eligible Texas families, it can be effectively free. If you don't have an award, you can still enroll and pay directly, then apply in a future window.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a Texas TEFA voucher for an online gifted school?

Yes. With an approved Texas Education Freedom Account, funds can pay tuition at a participating accredited private school, including an online K–8 gifted program like GT Anywhere. Eligibility and approval are determined by the state.

How much is the Texas Education Freedom Account worth?

The 2026-27 award is $10,474 per eligible student. Because GT Anywhere tuition is $10,400/year, an approved award can cover it in full.

Do I need an approved TEFA award before enrolling?

You need an approved award to use TEFA funds. You can enroll or apply to a school first, but the funding depends on state approval, so confirm current eligibility and windows on the official site.

What if I don't qualify for TEFA?

You can still enroll and pay tuition directly ($10,400/year for GT Anywhere) and apply in a future funding window. Check the official state site for the current cycle.
