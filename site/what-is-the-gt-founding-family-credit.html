How Does the GT Founding Family Credit Work?

SOURCE: GT School Evidence Library · Reading time 1 min · Last updated August 2026

Twenty-one families have asked about this, which means it is being mentioned in conversations and materials without a written explanation families can point to. That gap is what generates the repeat questions.

Open question · awaiting GT

Needed from GT: (1) What is the credit worth, and is it a percentage or a fixed amount? (2) How long does it last: one year, or for as long as the family stays enrolled? (3) Is it per child or per family? (4) Is it still available, and if there is a cutoff, what is it? (5) Does it stack with an ESA award or with sibling discounts? If the program has closed, this article should say so and redirect to what is currently available, rather than being deleted. Families are still asking.
