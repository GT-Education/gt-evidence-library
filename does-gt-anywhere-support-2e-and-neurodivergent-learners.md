# How Does GT Anywhere Support Twice-Exceptional and Neurodivergent Learners?

This is the largest unanswered cluster in GT's parent question data. Six related questions, roughly
sixty family-mentions between them, covering 2e support and its limits, IEP and 504 plans, dyslexia, ADHD,
autism and sensory needs, and platform accommodations like text-to-speech.

Some of the model's structure is genuinely well suited to a 2e child, and this is worth stating clearly.
A mastery-based platform lets a student run far ahead in a strength while taking the time they need in an
area of difficulty, without either being visible to a classroom of peers. The asynchronous schedule removes
the sensory and pacing load of a classroom day. The absence of class rank removes a comparison a 2e child
usually loses on.

But structure is not the same as support, and families deserve the difference spelled out.

[NEEDS-FACT: This one needs the most care, because the wrong answer here does real harm to a family making a
placement decision. Needed from GT:
(1) Does GT Anywhere accept students with an IEP or 504, and what happens to those plans on enrollment?
(2) Which accommodations are actually available in the platform: text-to-speech, read-aloud, extended time,
    reduced visual load, and are they available during MAP testing?
(3) What support does GT NOT provide? Speech therapy, OT, reading intervention, behavioral support. The
    limits need to be explicit, not implied.
(4) Is there a profile GT will decline, and what is it? An honest boundary here builds more trust than a
    blanket yes, and protects families from a placement that will not work.
(5) Does GT have staff with gifted and 2e training specifically?]
