# Can We Use Our State's ESA or Scholarship Funds for GT Anywhere?

GT Anywhere's tuition is set deliberately below the award level of several state education savings
account programs, so that for eligible families the program can be covered in full or close to it.

Texas families have the most established route, covered in detail in the existing guides on the Texas
Education Freedom Account. Families in other states are asking a different question: whether GT is an
approved provider where they live.

[NEEDS-FACT: Needed from GT, and this changes as programs are added, so it should carry a visible last-reviewed date:
(1) Which state ESA and scholarship programs is GT Anywhere currently an approved provider for? A current list.
(2) For each, what does a family select in that state's portal? The Texas answer of "GT School - Georgetown"
    is a known point of confusion, and 16 families asked about that one alone.
(3) What is the payment mechanism: does the state pay GT directly, or does the family pay and get reimbursed?
(4) What happens if an award arrives mid-year or covers only part of the tuition?]
